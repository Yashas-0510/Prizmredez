# Adversarial review brief — 5 scroll-film concepts

You are an adversarial creative director. You did NOT write these concepts.
Attack each one: specific, harsh, right. No rewrites, no full alternatives.
Under 700 words total. Headers per concept.

## Brand
Prizm Studio — AI creative agency (web design, ad creatives, AI UGC, social
marketing). Homepage hero = a scroll-film: one continuous AI-generated
cinematic sequence (Seedance 2.0 image-to-video, 5 clips x 5s = 25s, chained
start-frame to end-frame) scrubbed forward AND backward by the visitor's
scroll, dissolving into a near-black (#070708) page. The client rejected an
abstract eclipse/corona light-event ("not cinematic, no journey") and a
black-monolith salt-flat threshold film. They want dynamism with consequence:
things flying, assembling, detonating — cinematic, not screensaver.

## Hard constraints
- ONE declared vector per film (world/camera travels in OR out); one
  direction + one location + one lighting state per clip, or the model
  teleports mid-clip
- i2v truths: architecture, macro, liquids, glass, slow powder explosions =
  strong. Text/UI = broken. Glass fracture = decent ONLY when the object is
  simple and motion is slow. Fast chaotic motion = morph artifacts.
- First frame = hero with negative space for a huge headline.
  Last frame = dissolves into near-black.
- Scrubbed BOTH directions: state-change films (assemble/disassemble) read
  intentional on rewind; pure travel reads as "hallway on rewind".
- One white-out flash frame is allowed to hide one hard transition.

## The five concepts

### 1 — "SHARDS" (assembly)
Dark void. Hundreds of glass shards and slow-motion color-bomb powder charges
(cyan/violet/magenta/amber) hang scattered. Scroll: shards drawn inward,
converging, rotating into formation; powder ignites around them; they lock
into a crystalline prism; camera pushes into the finished glass (flash) then
dark interior then page. Forward = assemble, backward = disintegrate.
Vector: inward convergence.

### 2 — "SHATTER" (detonation)
A pristine glass prism floats in a soft aesthetic gradient backdrop, lit like
jewelry. Scroll: hairline cracks of spectral light race across it; it
detonates in slow motion; the shards turn toward the viewer and streak past
the lens like a meteor shower; the camera drifts through the emptying field
as the last shards streak away into darkness; page. Backward scrub =
un-shatter. Vector: outward from the prism.

### 3 — "BLACK TIDE" (the flood)
A pristine white studio room, soft daylight. A hairline crack opens in the
floor; black liquid glass wells up and floods in, spectral oil-slick patterns
turning on its surface; the camera rides the rising flood as the white room
is swallowed; the last white wall goes under; the surface settles into black
glass with one spectral slick; page. Vector: rising/inward.

### 4 — "THE DARKROOM"
Night: a fog-covered black water plain; a house-sized camera lens stands
half-submerged, moonlit. Camera pushes toward and through the front glass
(flash) into a darkroom interior; spectral images slowly develop up out of
black liquid in trays as the camera passes them; the camera sinks into the
blackest tray; page. Metaphor: the agency develops images out of darkness.
Vector: forward/into.

### 5 — "FIRST CRACK"
A seamless white monolithic surface fills the frame (architecture-scale).
A hairline crack appears; scroll drives it spidering wider; spectral light
bleeds through every fissure; the white shell sheds away in slow plates,
revealing a black prismatic crystalline core beneath; the core catches one
spectral flare as it turns; everything dims to black; page. Metaphor:
beneath every clean brief, our spectrum. Vector: inward reveal.

3. One wildcard arc, one sentence only.
